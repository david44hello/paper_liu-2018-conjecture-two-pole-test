# Manuscript and formal inputs

This map uses the frozen 37-page v2.03 manuscript (PDF MD5 `36bfa2cd185909b4e371afbd580693b9`). The line spans in CLAIM_MAP.tsv and PROOF_STEPS.tsv refer to the TeX or figure file named in each row. The 40 numbered entries, including three definitions and Proposition 6.1 as a computer hypothesis, are separate from the 849 passage records. Descriptions of diagrams and revised prose are summaries.

PROVED means that Lean derives the numbered statement using its three standard axioms alone. PROVED GIVEN names an additional explicit mathematical or numerical premise. A checked passage link may specialize a more general Lean theorem. A partial link covers only the clause described in the scope column. An unresolved row has no complete formal match. A finite-data check concerns the listed records, cutoffs or coordinates.

The numerical input is quantified over the exact 168 boxes used by the paper. The scalar hypothesis in Lemma 6.5 comes from the refereed article by İbrahimoğlu, §3.2, equation (13). The proof of the complex ellipse bound in Lemma 6.4 is independent of that scalar hypothesis. [Full scope and sources](WHAT_TO_READ.md)
